Privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Who can use CampusLink

CampusLink is for students who are 18 or older. By joining, you confirm that you are 18 or older.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored in encrypted form, and we cannot see it)
  • Your profile: first name, year, campus, major, gender, who you are looking for, a short bio, and an optional photo
  • Your activity: who you like, your matches, the people you block or report, and your chat messages

Who can see it

  • Other signed-in users can see your profile: name, photo, year, campus, major and bio.
  • Only you and the person you match with can read your chat.
  • Your email, password, and reports are never shown to other users.
  • Photos are stored in a public folder, so anyone who has the exact link to a photo can open it.
  • The people who run CampusLink can access stored data when needed to keep the site safe, for example when reviewing a report.

How we use it

We use your information only to run CampusLink: to show you to people who fit your choices, to make matches, and to deliver messages. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database, logins and photos). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

  • You can change your profile and photo anytime in the Profile tab.
  • You can report or block anyone at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it by emailing us.
